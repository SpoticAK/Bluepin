# Bluepin Medical / Health Disclaimer

**Effective Date:** 14 September 2026  
**Version:** 2.0

Bluepin provides tools that help users record, organize, and understand glucose readings, health reports, biomarkers, and other health information over time.

The information, trends, patterns, visualizations, comparisons, scores, and insights available through Bluepin, including **Bluepin Intelligence**, are provided for **general informational and health-management purposes only**.

This disclaimer explains the intended role and limitations of Bluepin. It does not replace the Bluepin Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Health Data Consent Notice, or AI Output Disclaimer.

---

## 1. Bluepin Is Not Medical Advice

Bluepin does not provide medical advice.

Information provided through Bluepin should not be interpreted as:

- a medical diagnosis;
- a clinical assessment;
- a prescription;
- a treatment recommendation;
- a recommendation to start, stop, or change medication;
- a recommendation to change insulin or other medication dosage;
- a substitute for medical testing;
- a substitute for professional medical judgment; or
- a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.

Bluepin is intended to help you organize and understand health information. It does not replace professional medical care.

Always seek appropriate professional medical advice regarding questions about your health, symptoms, diagnosis, medicines, treatment, or care.

---

## 2. Bluepin Is Not a Healthcare Provider

Bluepin is a health-information management and informational service.

Using Bluepin does not create a doctor-patient, clinician-patient, or other professional healthcare relationship between you and Bluepin.

Bluepin personnel do not become your healthcare providers merely because you use the service or submit health information.

Bluepin is not an emergency medical service.

---

## 3. Bluepin Intelligence

Bluepin Intelligence may analyze health information available in your Bluepin account to identify trends, patterns, relationships, and changes over time.

These outputs may be generated using automated analytical systems and AI models.

Bluepin Intelligence is intended to help you better understand information already available within your health history. Its outputs should not be treated as definitive clinical conclusions.

Automated insights may:

- be incomplete;
- contain inaccuracies;
- fail to identify a relevant health issue;
- identify a pattern that is not clinically significant;
- depend on incomplete or inaccurate information;
- be affected by errors in uploaded reports or extracted data; or
- differ from an assessment made by a qualified healthcare professional.

Do not make important medical or treatment decisions solely on the basis of a Bluepin Intelligence output.

For additional information, please review the **Bluepin AI Output Disclaimer**.

## 4. Glucose, Biomarker, and Health Trends

Graphs, historical comparisons, calculated values, trends, scores, and other health information displayed by Bluepin are intended to assist you in organizing and understanding your information.

A displayed trend, score, or pattern does not necessarily mean that:

- you have a particular medical condition;
- an existing condition has improved or worsened;
- a treatment is effective or ineffective;
- a medicine should be changed;
- a medical test is or is not required; or
- professional medical care is unnecessary.

Health information should be interpreted in the context of your individual circumstances by an appropriately qualified healthcare professional where medical interpretation is required.

Where relevant health information is available, Bluepin may help users observe changes or patterns associated with areas such as:

- kidney function;
- liver function;
- cardiovascular health;
- eye health; and
- other health indicators.

Such information is intended to support awareness and organization of your health history.

## 6. Accuracy of Information

Bluepin's outputs depend on the information available to the service.

You are responsible for checking that information you enter or upload is accurate and associated with the correct account.

Uploaded reports and extracted information may contain errors or omissions. These can result from, among other things:

- unclear or incomplete documents;
- OCR or extraction errors;
- incorrect values entered by a user;
- inconsistent units;
- different laboratory reference ranges; or
- incomplete health history.

If information displayed by Bluepin appears incorrect, verify it against the original report, source information, or healthcare professional before relying on it.

Bluepin is **not an emergency medical service** and should not be used to obtain emergency medical assistance.

If you believe you may be experiencing a medical emergency, severe symptoms, or an immediate threat to your health or safety, seek appropriate emergency medical assistance.

Do not delay emergency or professional medical care while waiting for information, analysis, or insights from Bluepin.
