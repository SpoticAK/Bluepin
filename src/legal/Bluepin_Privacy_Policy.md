# Bluepin Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 14 September 2026  
**Version:** 2.0

Bluepin ("**Bluepin**", "**we**", "**us**", or "**our**") respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal and health information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how Bluepin collects, uses, stores, processes, shares, protects, retains, and deletes information when you use our services.

This Policy applies to Bluepin's website, application, and related services, including:

- **bluepin.in**
- **app.bluepin.in**
- glucose tracking and management features
- health-report upload and analysis
- biomarker extraction and organization
- report comparison
- Health Highlights
- Health Score
- Health Canvas
- weight and BMI features
- Bluepin Intelligence and AI-powered insights

Bluepin is currently focused on helping users manage and understand diabetes-related and other health information they choose to provide.

## 1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Bluepin.

### 1.1 Account and Authentication Information

When you create or use a Bluepin account, we may collect or process:

- your name;
- email address;
- account identifiers;
- authentication information;
- information associated with your Google account when you use Google Sign-In;
- account creation and login information; and
- information relating to your account preferences and settings.

Bluepin currently uses **Firebase Authentication** and **Google Sign-In** for user authentication.

### 1.2 Health and Medical Information

When you use Bluepin's health features, you may provide information such as:

- glucose readings;
- HbA1c values;
- blood-test results;
- laboratory biomarkers;
- weight;
- height;
- BMI;
- blood pressure;
- cholesterol and lipid information;
- kidney and liver-related biomarkers;
- thyroid and vitamin-related biomarkers;
- urine-analysis information;
- medication information that you choose to provide;
- information contained in medical or laboratory reports; and
- other health information that you voluntarily provide.

Health reports may contain additional personal or medical information depending on their contents.

Health and medical information can be sensitive personal information. Bluepin treats such information accordingly.

### 1.3 Uploaded Reports and Files

If you upload a medical, laboratory, or other health-related report, Bluepin may process:

- the uploaded file;
- information contained in the file;
- extracted biomarkers;
- report dates;
- laboratory values;
- reference ranges;
- units; and
- other information necessary to organize and analyze the report.

Bluepin may retain uploaded reports and extracted information as part of your health history while your account remains active.

### 1.4 Technical and Usage Information

When you use Bluepin, we may automatically process information such as:

- IP address;
- browser and device information;
- operating-system information;
- session information;
- application and website interactions;
- technical events;
- error and diagnostic information;
- security events;
- performance information; and
- general usage information.

This information helps us operate, secure, maintain, and improve Bluepin.

## 5. Bluepin Intelligence and AI Processing

Bluepin uses automated analytical systems and AI models to provide certain informational insights.

Bluepin Intelligence may analyze information such as:

- laboratory reports;
- extracted biomarkers;
- glucose information;
- health-history information; and
- other information necessary for the requested feature.

### 5.1 Third-Party AI Processing

Bluepin currently uses **Google Gemini** through Google's AI services for certain AI-powered functionality.

Where an AI feature requires such processing, relevant information may be transmitted to the applicable Google AI service to generate the requested output.

Bluepin seeks to limit information sent for AI processing to information necessary for the relevant functionality.

### 5.2 AI Outputs

AI-generated information may contain errors, omissions, or inaccurate interpretations.

Bluepin Intelligence is intended to help users understand their health information and identify potential trends or patterns.

It is not intended to provide:

- a medical diagnosis;
- a prescription;
- emergency medical care;
- definitive treatment decisions; or
- a substitute for professional medical advice.

Important health decisions should be discussed with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
