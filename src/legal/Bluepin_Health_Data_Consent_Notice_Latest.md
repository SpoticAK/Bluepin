# Bluepin Health Data Consent Notice

**Effective Date:** 14 September 2026  
**Version:** 2.0

Bluepin helps you record, organize, compare, and understand health information over time.

Because Bluepin processes health and medical information, this Health Data Consent Notice explains what health information we process, why we process it, and the choices available to you.

This notice should be read together with the **Bluepin Privacy Policy**, **Bluepin Terms of Service**, **Medical / Health Disclaimer**, and **AI Output Disclaimer**.

---

Your health information is processed by:

**Bluepin**

Bluepin uses third-party infrastructure and technology providers where necessary to operate particular parts of the service. These providers may process information on Bluepin's behalf as described in the Privacy Policy.

## 2. What Health Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use and the information you choose to provide, Bluepin may process health and medical information including:

- glucose readings;
- photographs or images of glucometers or glucose readings;
- HbA1c information;
- blood reports and other medical or health reports;
- laboratory results;
- medication information;
- weight;
- height and BMI-related information;
- blood pressure;
- cholesterol information;
- kidney and liver markers;
- eye-health information;
- health-history information; and
- other health information contained in reports, records, images, or information that you voluntarily provide.

A medical report or uploaded file may contain additional personal or health information depending on its contents.

Bluepin may also process account information such as your name, email address, and other information necessary to associate health information with your account and provide the service.

---

## 3. How We Receive Health Information

Health information may be provided directly by you, including when you:

- enter health information into Bluepin;
- record glucose readings;
- enter weight or other measurements;
- upload a medical or laboratory report;
- upload an image containing health information; or
- otherwise submit health information through the service.

Bluepin's current product uses user-provided health reports and information. It does **not** currently operate a general live healthcare-provider integration ecosystem.

Do not assume that Bluepin can retrieve records directly from a doctor, hospital, laboratory, or other healthcare provider unless a particular integration or transfer method is explicitly made available to you.

---

## 4. Why We Process Your Health Information

With your consent, Bluepin may process your health information to:

- record and organize your health information;
- maintain your longitudinal health history;
- store and organize health reports;
- extract and structure information from reports;
- compare health information across reports or time periods;
- display trends and historical changes;
- identify patterns within the information you provide;
- generate Health Highlights and other informational analysis;
- provide Health Score or related product features where available;
- provide Health Canvas and related visualization features where available;
- generate Bluepin Intelligence insights;
- provide features you request;
- maintain, secure, and improve the reliability of the service;
- investigate technical errors or abuse;
- process deletion, privacy, and consent requests; and
- comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and security requirements.

Bluepin does not use personal health information for targeted advertising.

---

## 5. Bluepin Intelligence and Third-Party AI Processing

Bluepin Intelligence provides automated analysis of health information to help identify trends, patterns, and changes.

**Important:** Bluepin's current implementation uses **Google Gemini** to generate certain insights from information submitted for analysis.

For example, information from uploaded laboratory reports and extracted biomarker information may be included in an AI-processing request to Gemini.

Accordingly, by providing the health-data consent described in this notice, you acknowledge and consent to this processing where it is necessary to provide the applicable Bluepin Intelligence feature.

Bluepin will use appropriate technical and contractual controls applicable to its use of third-party AI services.

Bluepin Intelligence:

- may produce incomplete, inaccurate, or misleading outputs;
- depends on the quality and completeness of the information available to it;
- is not a medical diagnosis;
- does not replace a qualified healthcare professional; and
- should not be used as the sole basis for medical decisions.

For more information, see the **Bluepin AI Output Disclaimer**.

### Future AI development

Bluepin intends to develop its own analytical and machine-learning capabilities over time.

Any future use of health information for model training, research, or other purposes that materially changes the purposes described in this notice will be subject to appropriate privacy disclosures, consent, and other legal requirements before such processing is undertaken where required.

This notice does **not** state that Bluepin currently trains its own AI models using your health information.

## 6. Analytics and Advertising Technologies

Bluepin uses analytics technologies including:

- Google Analytics 4;
- Google Tag Manager; and
- Microsoft Clarity.

Bluepin's tracking implementations are designed so that health information is not intentionally transmitted to these analytics services.

Bluepin may also use advertising or remarketing technologies such as:

- Google Ads / Google Ads remarketing;
- Meta Pixel; and
- LinkedIn Insight Tag.

These technologies are intended for marketing measurement and advertising purposes and are not intended to receive your personal health information.

Bluepin does not intentionally send glucose readings, medical-report contents, laboratory results, medication information, Health Score information, Health Canvas health information, or other personal health information to advertising or analytics platforms.

Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential analytics, advertising, or remarketing technologies, Bluepin will obtain the applicable consent before activating those technologies.

More information is available in the **Bluepin Cookie & Tracking Policy**.

Your health-data consent is separate from:

## 9. Providing Health Data Is Your Choice

You may choose whether to provide health information to Bluepin.

However, if you do not provide or permit processing of health information required for a particular feature, Bluepin may be unable to provide that feature.

This may include features involving:

- glucose history;
- health-report processing;
- biomarker extraction;
- report comparison;
- health trends;
- Health Highlights;
- Health Score;
- Health Canvas; or
- Bluepin Intelligence.

## 10. Withdrawal of Health Data Consent

You may withdraw your consent to future health-data processing by contacting:

**connect@bluepin.com**

You may also delete your Bluepin account using the available account-deletion functionality.

Withdrawal of consent does not invalidate processing that occurred before withdrawal.

After withdrawal, Bluepin will stop processing that depends on the withdrawn consent where required by applicable law, unless Bluepin has another lawful basis or legal obligation to continue particular processing.

Withdrawal may prevent Bluepin from providing features that depend on the affected health information.

## 11. Account Deletion and Health Information

When you voluntarily delete your Bluepin account, Bluepin permanently removes the associated user data in accordance with its account-deletion process, subject to information that Bluepin is legally required or otherwise permitted to retain.

Bluepin does **not** apply an automatic 90-day deletion rule to inactive accounts.

The Privacy Policy provides additional information about deletion and retention.

## 14. Your Health Data and Medical Decisions

Bluepin is an informational health-management service.

Information displayed by Bluepin, including AI-generated insights, should not be treated as a diagnosis or as a substitute for professional medical advice.

Do not start, stop, increase, decrease, or otherwise change medication, insulin, treatment, or medical care solely because of information displayed by Bluepin.

If you have an urgent or emergency medical concern, seek appropriate medical assistance rather than relying on Bluepin.
