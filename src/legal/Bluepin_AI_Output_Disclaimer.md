# Bluepin AI Output Disclaimer

**Effective Date:** 14 September 2026  
**Version:** 2.0

Bluepin uses automated analytical systems and AI models to provide certain informational insights through **Bluepin Intelligence**.

This AI Output Disclaimer explains the nature and limitations of information generated by Bluepin's AI-powered features.

It should be read together with the **Bluepin Privacy Policy**, **Terms of Service**, and **Medical / Health Disclaimer**.

---

## 1. What Bluepin Intelligence Does

Bluepin Intelligence may analyze health information available in your Bluepin account to help identify potential:

- trends;
- patterns;
- changes over time;
- relationships between health information; and
- areas that may be useful for further discussion or attention.

Depending on the feature, the information analyzed may include glucose readings, laboratory reports, biomarkers, weight, BMI, and other health information that you provide.

The purpose of these outputs is to help you better understand the information in your health history.

---

## 2. AI-Generated Information Is Informational

Information generated by Bluepin Intelligence is provided for **informational and health-management purposes only**.

AI-generated outputs are not:

- medical advice;
- a medical diagnosis;
- a clinical assessment;
- a prescription;
- a treatment recommendation;
- a recommendation to start, stop, or change medication;
- a recommendation to change insulin or other treatment;
- emergency medical guidance; or
- a substitute for professional healthcare.

You should consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional when medical interpretation or advice is required.

---

## 3. AI Outputs May Contain Errors

AI systems can produce inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading information.

Bluepin Intelligence may:

- misunderstand information in a report;
- extract information incorrectly;
- overlook relevant information;
- identify a pattern that is not medically meaningful;
- fail to identify an important pattern;
- provide an incomplete explanation;
- produce an incorrect comparison;
- interpret information without sufficient context; or
- generate an output that does not apply to your individual circumstances.

An AI-generated output should therefore not be treated as a definitive statement about your health.

---

## 4. Your Health Information Matters

The quality of Bluepin Intelligence outputs depends partly on the quality and completeness of the information available to Bluepin.

If information is:

- missing;
- outdated;
- entered incorrectly;
- extracted incorrectly;
- associated with the wrong date;
- associated with the wrong unit; or
- otherwise incomplete,

the resulting AI output may also be inaccurate.

Where appropriate, verify important information against the original medical report or other reliable source.

---

## 5. No Sole Basis for Medical Decisions

You should not use a Bluepin Intelligence output as the sole basis for:

- diagnosing a medical condition;
- changing medication;
- changing insulin dosage;
- starting or stopping treatment;
- delaying medical care;
- deciding that professional care is unnecessary; or
- making another significant medical decision.

Important health decisions should be made with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

---

## 6. AI Does Not Replace Your Doctor

Bluepin Intelligence does not have access to all of the information that a healthcare professional may consider when evaluating your health.

A healthcare professional may consider:

- your symptoms;
- medical history;
- physical examination;
- current medications;
- family history;
- diagnostic tests;
- clinical context; and
- other information that may not be available to Bluepin.

For this reason, an AI-generated insight may differ from a healthcare professional's assessment.

Where there is a difference, seek professional medical advice.

Additional information about this processing is provided in the **Bluepin Privacy Policy**.

---

## 8. AI Model Changes

AI systems and models may change over time.

As Bluepin improves its AI-powered functionality, the behavior, capabilities, and limitations of AI-generated outputs may also change.

Bluepin may update, replace, or modify the AI models and systems used to provide particular features.

---

## 9. Emergencies

Bluepin Intelligence is not an emergency medical service.

Do not use Bluepin or wait for an AI-generated response when you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency or an immediate threat to your health or safety.

Seek appropriate emergency medical assistance immediately.

---

## 10. Review Important Information

Before relying on an AI-generated output, you should consider:

1. whether the underlying information is complete and accurate;
2. whether the output is consistent with the original report or information;
3. whether additional medical context may be relevant; and
4. whether you should discuss the information with a healthcare professional.

If an AI-generated output appears incorrect or concerning, do not rely on it as a medical conclusion.

---

## 11. No Guarantee of Accuracy

Bluepin works to make its AI-powered features useful and reliable, but does not guarantee that AI-generated outputs will always be:

- accurate;
- complete;
- current;
- medically appropriate;
- error-free; or
- suitable for your individual circumstances.

AI outputs are generated based on the information and systems available at the time the output is produced.
