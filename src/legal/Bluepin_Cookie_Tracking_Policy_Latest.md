## 1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small data files that websites and web applications may store on your browser or device.

Bluepin may also use similar technologies or technical identifiers for purposes such as:

- maintaining login sessions;
- authenticating users;
- protecting accounts and systems;
- remembering technical preferences;
- measuring website and application usage;
- understanding product performance;
- measuring marketing and advertising performance;
- creating or measuring advertising audiences where permitted; and
- diagnosing technical problems.

Different technologies may operate for different periods. Some may be deleted when a browser session ends, while others may remain for longer periods depending on their purpose and configuration.

## 9. Health Information and Tracking Technologies

Bluepin treats health and medical information as sensitive.

Bluepin does not intentionally use analytics or advertising technologies to transmit personal health information to third-party analytics or advertising platforms.

This includes information such as:

- glucose readings;
- HbA1c values;
- uploaded medical reports;
- laboratory results;
- medication information;
- blood pressure;
- cholesterol information;
- kidney or liver markers;
- Health Score information;
- Health Canvas health information; and
- Bluepin Intelligence health insights.

Bluepin also seeks to prevent health information from being revealed indirectly through:

- event names;
- event parameters;
- URL parameters;
- page titles;
- custom audiences;
- advertising conversion data; or
- other tracking metadata.

The Bluepin technical team should review tracking implementations before and after material changes to ensure that this Policy remains accurate.

Browser controls operate independently from any preference controls that Bluepin may provide.
